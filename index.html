    Automation rules

    HVAC cools above setpoint +0.5 °C or when humidity is over the limit. Lights switch on below min light. Blinds close when the room is 2 °C over setpoint. Ventilation runs at gas warning and is forced on at alarm.

    Automatic reports
    Automatic reports are off.
    Each report has the current readings, the lowest / average / highest since the last report, gas warnings, device states, energy and cost, the 30-minute forecast and the top recommendation. Reports are sent while this page is open and you are logged in. For reports with the laptop closed, set REPORT_EVERY_MIN in the ESP32 firmware.
    How to get a bot token and chat ID 
    1. In Telegram, open @BotFather → /newbot → choose a name.
   It replies with the bot token. Paste it above.
2. Open your new bot and press Start (send it "hi").
3. In a browser, open:
   https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates
   Find "chat":{"id":123456789 …  → that number is the chat ID.
4. Press Save, then Send test.
